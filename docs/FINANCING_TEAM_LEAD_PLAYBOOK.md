# Financing Team Lead Playbook — Lead + 2 RM

## Operating objective

The Team Lead should manage decisions, priorities, blockers, quality and staff capacity — not spend the day manually compiling status.

NEXUS should make the next action visible without requiring the Team Lead to open multiple files.

## Role design

### Team Lead
Owns:

- daily prioritisation;
- allocation and balancing;
- evidence-quality review;
- material judgement review;
- facility-structure review;
- recommendation challenge;
- blocker escalation;
- sign-off / return with comments;
- handoff readiness;
- coaching and quality trends.

The Team Lead should not become the default data-entry person.

### RM A and RM B
Each RM owns a portfolio of customer workspaces and completes:

- evidence intake and verification;
- non-extractable RM observations;
- financial/contract inputs requiring human confirmation;
- information-gap resolution;
- first-pass assessment;
- task completion evidence;
- response to Team Lead review comments.

## Default allocation logic

Assign work using four dimensions:

1. urgency / external deadline;
2. credit or policy risk;
3. current RM capacity;
4. continuity of customer knowledge.

Do not split one customer across both RMs unless there is a specific parallel-work reason. Preserve one accountable workspace owner.

## Daily rhythm

### 08:30 — 10-minute command review
Team Lead sees:

- critical cases;
- due today;
- overdue;
- blocked/waiting;
- human-review queue;
- RM capacity;
- new evidence;
- material changes.

Allocate only exceptions. Normal work remains with its existing owner.

### 10:30 — RM A review window
Review only items marked:

- needs_human_review;
- critical;
- policy exception;
- evidence conflict;
- recommendation ready.

Return structured comments directly to the task/section.

### 13:30 — RM B review window
Same rule.

### 15:00 — decision gate
Team Lead signs off, returns, escalates or marks a documented information gap.

### 16:30 — close-of-day control
NEXUS prepares:

- completed today;
- still blocked;
- external follow-ups;
- due tomorrow;
- Team Lead decisions outstanding;
- likely SLA risk;
- Team 2 handoff candidates.

## Blocked-task rule

Blocked does not mean idle.

When a task is blocked:

1. preserve the blocker and external dependency;
2. do not create a duplicate task;
3. surface the highest-value unblocked task within the same workspace;
4. only move to another customer when meaningful same-workspace work is exhausted.

## Team Lead dashboard

The first screen should answer in under 30 seconds:

- What is at risk?
- What requires my decision?
- Who is overloaded?
- What is blocked?
- What can be worked on instead?
- Which cases can move stage today?
- Which items are ready for document generation?
- Which cases are ready for Team 2 handoff?

## Performance model

Avoid measuring financing staff only by eventual disbursement.

Track controllable productivity and quality:

- complete assessment packages;
- evidence completeness;
- first-pass quality;
- turnaround time by stage;
- information-gap closure;
- Team Lead return rate;
- recommendation readiness;
- approval conversion;
- approval-to-LO handoff time;
- avoidable rework;
- duplicate data entry eliminated.

Disbursement remains an outcome metric but should not erase productive work when pricing, customer choice or later-stage conditions prevent drawdown.

## Coaching signal

NEXUS should identify repeated return reasons by RM:

- unsupported fact;
- incomplete analysis;
- calculation error;
- weak mitigant;
- missing policy reference;
- incomplete evidence;
- unclear recommendation.

Use trends for coaching, not punitive automation.

## Delegation boundary

The Team Lead may delegate preparation. The system must not silently delegate the human accountability for material credit judgement or approval.
